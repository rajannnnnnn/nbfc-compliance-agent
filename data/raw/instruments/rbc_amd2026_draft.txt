CHAPTER V - RECOVERY OF LOANS AND ENGAGEMENT OF RECOVERY AGENTS (DRAFT)

100W. Permitted contact hours (draft). A regulated entity or its recovery agent shall contact
a borrower for recovery purposes only between 0700 hours and 2000 hours, a wider window than
proposed in the version reported as notified.

100X. Third-party contact (draft). A regulated entity or its recovery agent shall not contact
any relative, friend, colleague or employer of the borrower for the purpose of intimidating
or shaming the borrower into payment, in terms materially similar to the notified text but
retained here only for forward-looking review, never for citation.

100Y. Grievance officer details (draft). Every notice, letter or other communication issued
in connection with recovery shall disclose the name, telephone number and email address of
the grievance officer, a narrower disclosure requirement than the notified text's additional
requirement of a postal address.

100Z. Review clause (draft). These provisions shall be reviewed two years after commencement
and may be revised having regard to representations received from regulated entities,
recovery agents, and consumer protection bodies during the public comment period that
preceded the notified version of this Chapter.

Explanatory note (draft only, not part of the operative text). This draft was circulated for
comment on 20 May 2026 and differs from the notified text in the contact-hour window, the
scope of the third-party contact prohibition, and the grievance-officer disclosure
requirement. It is retained in this corpus solely so that forward-looking review can compare
the draft against the notified and final text side by side; the evaluation suite asserts that
no path under this instrument is ever cited in a persisted finding, in any citation role.
