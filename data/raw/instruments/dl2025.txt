CHAPTER III - CONDUCT REQUIREMENTS

6. Nodal grievance redressal officer. Every regulated entity and lending service provider
shall designate a nodal grievance redressal officer for dealing with digital lending related
complaints and shall prominently publish the details of the officer on its website and within
the digital lending application.

8. Disclosure and recovery agent requirements. Every regulated entity shall ensure that any
recovery agent engaged for collections discloses their identity and the identity of the
regulated entity on whose behalf they are calling, before the first point of contact with the
borrower.
i. The regulated entity shall ensure that the Key Facts Statement is issued in terms of
instructions contained in the Key Facts Statement circular before execution of the loan
agreement.
ii. The Annual Percentage Rate shall be disclosed prominently in the Key Facts Statement and
shall be computed on the same basis across all disclosure documents.

9. Disbursal and repayment routing.
i. All loan disbursals under a digital lending arrangement shall be made directly into the
bank account of the borrower, without any pass-through account of the lending service
provider or any third party.
ii. All loan repayments shall be collected back only into the bank account of the regulated
entity, without any pass-through account of the lending service provider.
iii. Any fees or charges payable to a lending service provider in the digital lending
arrangement shall be paid directly by the regulated entity and shall not be charged to the
borrower.

10. Cooling-off period. The borrower shall be given an explicit option to exit the digital
loan by paying the principal and proportionate annual percentage rate without penalty during
the cooling-off period. No prepayment penalty shall be levied for exercise of this option.
Note: The cooling-off period shall not be less than one day.

11. Grievance officer and escalation. The Key Facts Statement shall disclose the name,
telephone number and email address of the grievance redressal officer designated to deal with
digital lending complaints, and shall disclose that a complaint not resolved within thirty
days may be escalated in accordance with the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

13. Data privacy and biometric information. Where data of the borrower is processed at a
location outside India, such data shall be deleted from servers outside India and stored only
in India within a period of twenty-four hours. No biometric information shall be collected or
stored by the regulated entity, the lending service provider or the digital lending app,
except where explicitly permitted under law.

17. Outsourcing arrangements. Outsourcing of any core function to a lending service provider
shall not diminish the regulated entity's obligations, and the regulated entity shall remain
fully responsible for conduct undertaken on its behalf.
