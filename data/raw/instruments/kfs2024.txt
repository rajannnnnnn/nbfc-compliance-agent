CHAPTER I - KEY FACTS STATEMENT

1. Validity of the Key Facts Statement. The Key Facts Statement shall bear a unique proposal
number and shall remain valid for a period of not less than three working days for loans with
a tenor of seven days or more.

2. Reproduction in the loan agreement. The particulars contained in the Key Facts Statement
shall be reproduced as a summary box forming part of the loan agreement, and shall not merely
be referenced.

3. Language and format. The Key Facts Statement shall be provided in a standardised format,
in a language understood by the borrower, and shall be furnished free of cost before the
execution of the loan agreement.

4. Machine-readability. Where the loan is sanctioned through a digital channel, the Key Facts
Statement shall additionally be furnished in a machine-readable format to support
verification by the borrower and by supervisory functions.

=== ANNEX A ===
Sl No    Item    Description
1. Loan proposal / account number    Unique identifier assigned by the lender for this loan
2. Type of loan    Personal, gold, vehicle, business, consumer durable or other
3. Sanctioned loan amount    Amount sanctioned under the loan agreement, in rupees
4. Disbursal schedule    Manner and stages in which the sanctioned amount is disbursed
5. Loan term    Tenor of the loan expressed in months or days
6. Cooling off / look-up period    Period during which the borrower may exit without penalty, not less than one day
7. Instalment details    Amount and periodicity of instalments payable by the borrower
8. Interest rate type and reset periodicity    Fixed or floating, and frequency of reset if floating
9. Annual Percentage Rate    All-in annualised cost of credit inclusive of all fees and charges
10. Details of contingent charges    Charges that may become payable in specified contingencies

2. Grievance officer contact    Escalation contact for unresolved grievances
3. Grievance officer phone and email    Telephone number and email address of the grievance officer
