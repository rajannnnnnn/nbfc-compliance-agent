CHAPTER III - DISCLOSURE

29. Annual Percentage Rate. The Annual Percentage Rate disclosed in the sanction letter and
loan agreement shall be identical to the Annual Percentage Rate disclosed in the Key Facts
Statement, computed on the same basis.

CHAPTER IV - FAIR PRACTICE IN SERVICING AND RECOVERY

30. Penal charges.
(1) Penal charges for non-compliance with material terms and conditions of the loan contract
shall not be levied in the form of penal interest that is added to the rate of interest
charged on the advance.
(2) Penal charges shall not be capitalised, and no further interest shall be computed on such
charges.

35. Release of documents. The regulated entity shall release all original movable or
immovable property documents and remove charges registered with any registry within a period
of 30 days after full repayment or settlement of the loan.

36. Manner of release. Original documents shall be released either at the branch where the
loan account was serviced or at any other office of the regulated entity chosen by the
borrower.

37. Notice of release. The regulated entity shall provide the borrower a notice of the date
and place of release along with the return of the documents.

38. Joint borrowers. Where there are joint borrowers, the regulated entity shall release
documents to any of the joint borrowers authorised to receive them.

39. Compensation for delay. In case of delay in releasing original documents or removing
charges beyond the period specified in paragraph 35, the regulated entity shall compensate
the borrower at the rate of ₹5,000 for each day of delay attributable to the regulated entity.

40. Lost documents. Where original documents are lost or damaged, the regulated entity shall
assist the borrower in obtaining duplicate documents, and the additional time so taken, not
exceeding thirty days over and above the period specified in paragraph 35, shall not be
counted towards the delay for the purpose of compensation under paragraph 39.

H. Microfinance conduct requirements

45. Contact hours for microfinance borrowers. Recovery agents and staff of the regulated
entity shall not contact microfinance borrowers otherwise than between 0800 hours and 1900
hours.
