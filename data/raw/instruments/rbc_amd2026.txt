CHAPTER V - RECOVERY OF LOANS AND ENGAGEMENT OF RECOVERY AGENTS

100E. Applicability. This Chapter applies to the recovery of loans and the engagement of
recovery agents by every regulated entity, in addition to and not in derogation of the
general fair practice obligations elsewhere in these Directions.

100F. Certification of recovery agents. Every recovery agent engaged by a regulated entity
shall hold a valid certification recognised for the purpose, subject to a transition
allowance for agents already engaged as on the date these provisions commence.

100I. Prior intimation before a visit. A regulated entity or its recovery agent shall provide
the borrower at least one day's prior intimation before the first visit to the borrower's
residence or place of work for the purpose of recovery.

100N. Recording and retention of calls. Every call made to a borrower for collection or
recovery purposes shall be recorded, and the recording shall be preserved for a period of not
less than six months from the date of the call.

100Q. Preconditions for device restriction. A regulated entity shall not restrict access to a
device financed under the loan agreement unless the device was itself financed by that loan,
the account is sixty days or more past due, and the borrower has been given a cure notice of
twenty-one days followed, on continued non-payment, by a further cure notice of seven days.

100R. Cure notice sequence. The twenty-one day cure notice and the seven day cure notice
referred to in paragraph 100Q shall be issued strictly in that order, each after the
preceding notice period has run its course without payment.

100S. Compensation for wrongful restriction. Where a device is restricted in breach of
paragraph 100Q, or restoration of access is wrongfully delayed after the borrower has
remedied the default, the regulated entity shall compensate the borrower at the rate of ₹250
for every hour of wrongful delay in restoration.

100W. Permitted contact hours. Save as otherwise provided for microfinance borrowers, a
regulated entity or its recovery agent shall contact a borrower for recovery purposes only
between 0800 hours and 1900 hours.

100X. Third-party contact and social media. A regulated entity or its recovery agent shall
not contact any relative, friend, colleague or employer of the borrower for the purpose of
intimidating or shaming the borrower into payment, and shall not make any disclosure about
the borrower's account on a social media platform.

100Y. Grievance officer details on recovery communications. Every notice, letter or other
communication issued in connection with recovery shall disclose the name, telephone number,
email address and postal address of the grievance officer to whom the borrower may address a
complaint.
